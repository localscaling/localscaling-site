Why Jacksonville is different

One city spread across a whole county

Jacksonville and Duval County merged into one government decades ago, so the city reaches from the farmland out west to the Atlantic. On paper it is one place. In search it is dozens. A family in Mandarin types Mandarin, and a homeowner in Arlington types Arlington. An insurance agency in Riverside will not show on the map for someone in Oceanway, however strong the profile.

Someone in Neptune Beach types Neptune Beach. Jacksonville is the place over the bridge.

The St. Johns River runs through the middle, and the bridges decide where people are willing to drive. Locals sort the city into the Northside, the Westside, the Southside, Arlington, and the Beaches. Atlantic Beach, Neptune Beach, and Jacksonville Beach are their own cities, and Ponte Vedra Beach and Nocatee to the south sit in St. Johns County. Orange Park and Fleming Island to the southwest are in Clay County. Each one searches by its own name.

Every hurricane season, people here watch the forecast and start making calls. Roofers, tree crews, insurance agents, and restoration companies all get busy around a storm, and so does anyone who fixes what water and wind leave behind. Google decides who shows up on those days from what your profile and site looked like before them.

Two spots on one page

The map gets the call. The listing under it closes the deal.

When someone in the Jacksonville area searches for what you do, Google shows the map first and the regular results under it, and both are drawn around the neighborhood or town they are searching from. A business that holds a spot in both, in that place, gets the call.

We work both. Your Google Business Profile wins the map. Your website wins the listing under it. The audit checks where you stand in each.

Questions

What Jacksonville owners ask us

Do you need to be in Jacksonville to rank my business here?

No. What Google weighs is your location, your profile, your reviews, and your site. We run Jacksonville campaigns remotely and the work is the same.

Can one profile cover the whole city and the Beaches?

No single listing reaches from Oceanway to Ponte Vedra Beach. You win the area around your address first, then reach further with neighborhood and town pages as the profile gets stronger.

How long until I see results?

Usually three to six months to start seeing big results. Some parts of town move sooner than others, and we tell you which yours is before you commit.

Our office is on the Westside and we want Southside customers. Is the river a problem?

It makes the map harder, since Google weighs distance. Pages for the Southside neighborhoods you cover, a service area that includes them, and reviews from customers there are how you reach across. A second listing needs a real staffed office, and a virtual office or a mailbox can get the whole profile suspended.

We are in Orange Park. Should our pages say Orange Park or Jacksonville?

Both, each on its own page. People who live there search for Orange Park, and people across the county line search for Jacksonville. Your profile keeps your real address, and your site gets a page for each name you want to win.
