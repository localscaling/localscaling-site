Why San Jose is different

A dozen cities with no space between them

San Jose is the biggest city in the valley, and it shares its edges with Santa Clara, Campbell, Milpitas, Cupertino, and Los Gatos. You can drive from one into the next without seeing a sign. The people who live there know exactly where the line is. A homeowner in Campbell types Campbell, and a family in Cupertino types Cupertino.

On a map the valley looks like one long city. In the search box it is a list of names.

Inside San Jose the neighborhoods carry their own names too. Willow Glen and the Rose Garden sit west of downtown, Berryessa and Alum Rock are on the east side, and Evergreen and Almaden Valley climb toward the hills. The city is wide, and traffic on 101, 280, and 87 makes a short trip on the map feel long, so most people pick someone close. A physical therapist in Evergreen rarely shows on the map for a patient in North San Jose, however strong the profile.

A lot of households here speak a language other than English at home, and plenty of people search in Spanish, Vietnamese, Chinese, or Tagalog. A business that can serve them in their language should say so where they look first.

Two spots on one page

The map gets the call. The listing under it closes the deal.

When someone in San Jose or a nearby city searches for what you do, Google shows the map first and the regular results under it, and both are drawn around the place they are searching from. A business that holds a spot in both, for that place, gets the call.

We work both. Your Google Business Profile wins the map. Your website wins the listing under it. The audit checks where you stand in each.

Questions

What San Jose owners ask us

Do you need to be in San Jose to rank my business here?

No. Google weighs your location, your profile, your reviews, and your site, and none of those depend on where we work from. We run San Jose campaigns remotely and report to you every month.

How long until I see results?

Usually three to six months to start seeing big results. Some parts of the valley move faster than others, and we tell you which before you commit.

Can one profile cover San Jose and the whole valley?

No single listing reaches from Milpitas to Morgan Hill. You win the area around your address first, then reach further with area pages as the profile gets stronger. A second listing needs a real staffed office, and a virtual office or a mailbox can get the whole profile suspended.

We are in Campbell. Should our pages say Campbell or San Jose?

Both, each on its own page. People nearby search for Campbell, and people further out search for San Jose. Your profile keeps your real address, and your site gets a page for each name you want to win.

Should we have pages in Spanish or Vietnamese?

Only if someone on your team can serve customers in that language. If they can, a page in that language reaches the people who search in it, and your profile can tell them they are welcome to call.
