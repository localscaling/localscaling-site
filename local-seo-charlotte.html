Why Charlotte is different

A metro that sits on a state line

Charlotte grew fast, and most of that growth landed outside the old city. Locals call downtown Uptown, and the neighborhoods around it each have their own name and their own crowd. A family in Myers Park and a young couple in NoDa live ten minutes apart and search for different businesses.

Someone in Fort Mill types Fort Mill. Charlotte is the city across the line where they work.

Head south on I-77 and you are in South Carolina within half an hour. Fort Mill, Tega Cay, and Rock Hill sit in York County, and Indian Land sits in Lancaster County. People there shop in Charlotte and search close to home. The map pays no attention to the state line, and the searcher still types their own town.

To the north, the towns around Lake Norman have filled in with families: Huntersville, Cornelius, Davidson, and Mooresville. To the east sit Matthews and Mint Hill, and Concord is up past the speedway. I-485 loops around the city, and plenty of people treat it as the line they will not cross for a service call. A roofing company in Steele Creek rarely shows on the map for a homeowner in Cornelius, however strong the profile.

Questions

What Charlotte owners ask us

Do you need to be in Charlotte to rank my business here?

No. What Google weighs is your location, your profile, your reviews, and your site. We run Charlotte campaigns remotely and the work is the same.

We are in Charlotte and want customers in Fort Mill and Rock Hill. Does the state line matter?

Google ignores it. Distance still matters, so you reach across with a page for each South Carolina town you serve, a service area that includes them, and reviews from customers there.

How long until I see results?

Usually three to six months to start seeing big results. Some parts of town move sooner than others, and we tell you which yours is before you commit.

Can one profile cover the whole metro?

No single listing reaches from Mooresville to Rock Hill. You win the area around your address first, then reach further with town pages as the profile gets stronger. A second listing needs a real staffed office, and a virtual office or a mailbox can get the whole profile suspended.

We are in Huntersville. Should our pages say Huntersville or Charlotte?

Both, each on its own page. People nearby search for Huntersville or Lake Norman, and people further south search for Charlotte. Your profile keeps your real address, and your site gets a page for each name you want to win.
