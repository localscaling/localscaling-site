Why Columbus is different

A city stitched together from a lot of names

Columbus grew by taking in land around it, so the city line wanders. A Columbus mailing address can sit in a township, or right beside a suburb with its own city hall and its own schools. People here know which side they live on, and they search by that name.

A family in Westerville types Westerville. Columbus is where they go for a game at the Horseshoe.

Inside the city, the neighborhoods carry their own names too. Short North, German Village, Clintonville, Victorian Village, and the University District around Ohio State each have their own crowd. The campus blocks turn over with every school year, and the people moving in need a dentist, a mechanic, and a landlord who answers the phone.

I-270 loops the city, and locals call it the Outerbelt. Plenty of people treat it as the edge of how far they will look. Dublin and Hilliard sit to the northwest, Westerville and Gahanna to the northeast, Grove City to the south, and Pickerington to the east. The growth keeps pushing north into Delaware County toward Powell and Delaware, and northeast past New Albany. A clinic in Grove City rarely shows on the map for a patient in Powell, however strong the profile.

The weather sets the busy weeks for a lot of trades. Central Ohio winters freeze and thaw, spring rain finds every weak basement wall, and summers run humid. Furnaces, sump pumps, roofs, and gutters all get their rush, and Google decides who shows up in it from what your profile and site looked like before it started.

Questions

What Columbus owners ask us

Do you need to be in Columbus to rank my business here?

No. What Google weighs is your location, your profile, your reviews, and your site. We run Columbus campaigns remotely and the work is the same.

Our mailing address says Columbus but we are in a township. What goes on the profile?

Your real street address, exactly as the post office has it. Your pages then name the area the way locals say it, so people searching for your township or the suburb next door still find you.

How long until I see results?

Usually three to six months to start seeing big results. Some suburbs move sooner than others, and we tell you which yours is before you commit.

Can one profile cover the whole Outerbelt and beyond?

No single listing reaches from Delaware to Grove City. You win the area around your address first, then reach further with suburb pages as the profile gets stronger. A second listing needs a real staffed office, and a virtual office or a mailbox can get the whole profile suspended.

We are in Dublin. Should our pages say Dublin or Columbus?

Both, each on its own page. People nearby search for Dublin, and people further out search for Columbus. Your profile keeps your real address, and your site gets a page for each name you want to win.
